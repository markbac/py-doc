## Introduction {.abstract}

Long Meadow is owned by 820 local members. It sells its electricity to the grid and its profits fund insulation for the village. This is how it did in its third year.

## 6. The path: from sun to socket

```flow
[Panels|spark] -DC 1,500 V-> [Inverters|brand] -AC 690 V-> [Transformer|second] -33 kV-> [Grid|grey]
[Inverters|brand] -data-> [Monitoring]
```

Each inverter turns the panels' direct current into alternating current at 690 V; the site transformer lifts it to 33 kV for the local network.
